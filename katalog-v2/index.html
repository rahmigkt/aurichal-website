Artist represented in Toronto

Meet the Artist Behind Our Toronto Collection.

Elena Varela · Painter and ceramic artist · Represented in Toronto

“I am interested in what remains when a landscape becomes a memory: a color, a line, the weight of the earth.”

Elena grew up between Cuenca’s Andean landscape and her grandmother’s pottery workshop. After studying fine arts in Madrid, she returned to Ecuador to develop a quiet practice in mineral pigment, raw linen, and hand-built clay. Casa Áurea presents her original paintings and ceramics to collectors, homeowners, and interior designers across Toronto and Canada.
